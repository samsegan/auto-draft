What Auto Draft is

Auto Draft is a private tool run by Segan Music. It uploads Segan Music's own clips and photos to TikTok as drafts, so they can be finished and posted in the TikTok app.

Who may use it

Auto Draft is not a public service. It may be used only by Segan Music and by TikTok accounts that Segan Music has added itself. Nobody else may connect an account to it.

What it does with a connected account

With the account holder's approval, Auto Draft uploads content to that account's TikTok drafts. It does not publish anything. Every post is reviewed, edited and published by the account holder inside TikTok.

Only content that the account holder owns or has the right to use is uploaded. The account holder is responsible for what they choose to post.

Ending access
