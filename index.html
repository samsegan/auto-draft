Auto Draft

A private tool by Segan Music

What it is

Auto Draft sends Segan Music's own song clips and photos to Segan Music's own TikTok account as drafts. The drafts arrive in the TikTok app's inbox, where they are finished and posted by hand.

What it is not

It is not a public service. Nobody can sign up for it, and it only works with TikTok accounts that Segan Music has added itself. It never posts anything on its own.

How it works

  • The account holder signs in with TikTok once and approves the upload permission.
  • Auto Draft uploads a clip through TikTok's Content Posting API as a draft.
  • TikTok notifies the account holder, who opens the draft, edits it and decides whether to post.
